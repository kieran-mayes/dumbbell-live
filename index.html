Bouncing dumbbell, live

Drop a rigid dumbbell on a frictionless floor and watch every bounce. Each bounce adds a point to the Poincaré section; the height trace redraws the paper’s Fig. 2 as it happens.

Units: lengths in ball diameters D, time in √(D/g), mass in m. As in the paper, the ends are treated as points at the ball centers, so the floor is drawn half a ball below them. Physics matches dumbbell_sim.py: exact parabolic flights, a contact search that provably cannot skip a hit, and an impulse at the lower end.
